About Us

Learn more about the IEEE-RGIT Student Branch.

Who We Are

The IEEE Student Branch at Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Technology (RGIT) serves as a dynamic hub for engineering enthusiasts. We foster technical competence, professional networking, and ethical innovation across multiple domains.

With a specialized focus on VLSI and Embedded Systems, our community provides students with rigorous practical exposure, bridging core academic curriculum with real-world industry demands.

Core Objectives

  • Promoting technical excellence in hardware & software.
  • Hosting research seminars and bootcamps.
  • Mentoring teams for technical showcases.

What We Do

Engaging activities designed to build real engineering capability.

VLSI & Embedded Systems

Hands-on training on FPGA boards, microcontroller programming, and circuit simulation.

Technical Workshops

Regular coding labs covering C++, Python, system architecture, and modern toolchains.

Innovation Projects

Collaborative student projects solving real-world challenges through automation and IoT.
